Mobile app · Acoustic Connect

Pulse, in your pocket

A mobile companion that tells you what changed before you finish your coffee.

The situation

Acoustic Connect is built for a desk and a big monitor. The people who need it most are on a factory floor, on the train, or between meetings with about ninety seconds to spare.

The task

Bring the best of Connect to a phone without shrinking the dashboard. An exec and an analyst also don't want the same first number, so one fixed feed was never going to work.

What I did

I designed a standalone iOS app. It opens on a written AI Brief, lets each person reorder their own metric tiles, and links every number down to the contacts behind it. Resonate, a chat assistant, answers the follow-up question right there.

What happened

People now check in more than three times as often. Most weekly users ask Resonate a follow-up, and a notification turns into an understood insight in under two minutes.
