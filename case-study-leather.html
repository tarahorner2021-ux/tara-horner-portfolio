Mobile shop · Luke Pope Leather

A shop that fits on a workbench

Wallets, belts and holsters built to order by one maker. This is the storefront sized for him.

The situation

Luke makes leather goods on a single bench. Orders came in over texts and Instagram DMs, and customers had no way to see what was possible before asking.

The task

Give him a real shop that one person can run from his phone, including a way to take custom work and a way to show the work itself.

What I did

A single toggle splits Ready to ship from Coming soon, each with a real date. A custom order form works from a description and a reference photo. A gallery shows finished pieces and the bench, and orders can be tracked.

What happened

The shop went from DMs to something a customer can browse, request from and follow. It launched with four flows and twelve pieces, and it is still a one-person shop.

The decisions that mattered

Sell the making, not just the thing

Two shop statesReady to ship and Coming soon live together, so nothing is sold as in stock when it isn't.
Process as proofTool layouts and half-built pieces show the maker, which matters when the thing doesn't exist yet.
A form, not a configuratorDescribe it, attach a reference. That matches how Luke already works.
Updates like a text threadOrder tracking reads the way a message from Luke would.
